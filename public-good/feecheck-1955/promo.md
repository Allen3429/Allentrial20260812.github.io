【English】
Asked to pay a transfer / renewal / “job purchase” fee? Pause before paying.
Taiwan MOL guidance updated Sep. 10: brokers may not charge migrant workers brokerage fees for continued employment or employer transfer.
Monthly service fee is separate: contract + actual service + no advance collection; caps NT$1,800 / 1,700 / 1,500 by work year.
