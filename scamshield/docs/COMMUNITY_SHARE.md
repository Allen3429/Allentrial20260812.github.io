## Recommended post

Built **ScamShield — Trust Under Attack** at Perxona Taipei Hackathon 2026.

Scams no longer just fake facts — they can fake people. ScamShield is a Perxona-powered web game for practicing identity verification under pressure. A synthetic AI avatar claims to be a trusted person or authority, and the player must interrupt the manipulation, switch to a trusted verification channel, and follow the correct security process.

Highlights:
- Perxona 3D avatar + voice + motion
- **BREAK THE SPELL**: interrupt the avatar mid-speech
- 3-stage / 12-round campaign
- enterprise scenarios: CEO fraud, vendor account change, MFA fatigue, payroll change
- Recovery Checks after unsafe decisions
- safety-bounded scenarios: no real-person cloning or outbound scam capability

Live demo: https://allen3429.github.io/Allentrial20260812.github.io/scamshield/

Source: https://github.com/Allen3429/Allentrial20260812.github.io/tree/main/scamshield
